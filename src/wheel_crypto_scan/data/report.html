How to read this report

Each row is one wheel. The table shows the cryptographic evidence found inside it: which primitive families and libraries it carries and how they link, then what that evidence means through the FIPS lens: the class badge, the relations, and the reasons behind them.

It reports evidence, not a decision. Nothing here says a wheel is fine to ship on a FIPS host. A human makes that call; this report exists to make it an informed one.

Click a row for the full evidence: findings, binaries, wheel metadata, errors and the raw record. Click a column name to sort. Filter by class, OpenSSL linkage or "needs review only", or type part of a filename or rule id in the search box. Every column and filter has a "?" beside it, and Reference (in Help) explains every value.

Where to start: the "needs review" chip. Those are the wheels a rule asked a human to look at. OPAQUE means the tool could not read enough to say, not that nothing is there.
